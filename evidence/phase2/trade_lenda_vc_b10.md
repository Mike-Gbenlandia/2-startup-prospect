## About this round

USD 300,000 from Village Capital's Africa Ecosystem Catalysts Facility (USD 4M pilot facility managed by Village Capital, funded by FMO and the Netherlands Enterprise Agency, RVO). Amount per Village Capital's own press release ('The Facility is investing USD 300K'; investor-reported, dated 7 Sep 2026); part of a combined USD 450K first Nigeria deployment shared with AirSmat (USD 150K, funding 1507316).
