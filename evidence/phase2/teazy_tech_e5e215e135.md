The Orange Corners Innovation Fund (OCIF) has backed Teazy Tech with an $11K pre-seed investment, signaling support for the startup's mission to modernize classrooms.

Teazy Tech, an acronym for Teach Easy Technology, is building a community of educators. Its goal is to equip teachers to seamlessly integrate the latest educational technology tools, fostering innovative professionals who impact their classrooms and beyond.
