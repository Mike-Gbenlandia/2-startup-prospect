## About Tryctor

Tryctor makes tractors and farm implements for smallholder farmers. Its flagship Tryctor is a three-wheeled mini tractor adapted from a motorcycle design that can be fitted with implements, carry produce and power a generator; it was developed by Bespoke Design Concepts Limited with Nigeria's National Centre for Agricultural Mechanization.

The company is based in Ilorin, Kwara State. Its inventor, Olufemi Odeleye, was in the 2016 Africa Prize for Engineering Innovation cohort. Tryctor received US$4,000 at the NSIA Prize for Innovation 4.0 demo day in October 2026.
