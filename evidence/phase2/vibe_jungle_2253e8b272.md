Vibe Jungle is an AI entertainment company that makes films, series, images, music, podcasts, advertising and games for artists, creators, filmmakers and brands.

Founders

- Keziah Aboribo · co-founder and CEO

WHY IT MATTERS

Vibe Jungle targets the high cost of studio time, production and mastering that can leave Nigerian musicians struggling to profit, while giving smaller creative teams access to film, music, image and other production tools. Its $400,000 founder-led investment is being used to build an AI studio, original entertainment and intellectual property, and a licensable music catalogue, betting that human-led production plus AI can make professional work more affordable and scalable in Africa.
