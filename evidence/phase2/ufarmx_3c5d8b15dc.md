## About UfarmX

[![Screenshot of UfarmX website](https://liners.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fassets.liners.com%2Fstorage%2Fv1%2Fobject%2Fpublic%2Fmedia%2Ftools%2Fufarmx%2Fscreenshot.webp%3Fv%3D1772635895725&w=3840&q=75)](https://www.ufarmx.com/?utm_source=liners.com)

UfarmX is an agtech platform for African smallholder farmers and agribusiness buyers, combining collateral-free financing, farm input credit, and traceable market access.

Key capabilities include:

- **AI-driven credit scoring** using farm and geospatial data (including satellite imagery and farm history) to support **collateral-free financing** through partner financial institutions
- **Inputs on credit** such as quality seeds and fertilizers, paired with **technical support** and climate-smart guidance to improve farm productivity
- **Blockchain-based digital records** (built on Polkadot) with **QR codes for traceability**, helping buyers verify provenance and maintain transparent supply chains
- **Direct market linkages** with secured purchase orders and more consistent pricing, designed to reduce reliance on middlemen

Available on **Web**, **Android**, **iOS** and **API**.

Built for a **B2B2C** audience, it serves **smallholder farmers** as end users, while supporting **agribusinesses and produce buyers** with supply visibility and sourcing of verified produce.

Notable in Africa for active use in **Nigeria and Senegal**, with reported pilots indicating higher yields and revenues and default rates under 10 percent, plus partnerships and program participation including **Techstars**, **IFC World Bank**, and public-sector and NGO collaborators. Founded in **2019**, it focuses on formalising informal farming data to help close the region’s agricultural financing gap.

## Frequently Asked Questions About UfarmX

### What is UfarmX and how does it work?

UfarmX is a B2B agribusiness platform that connects smallholder farmers to financing, markets, and supply chain solutions while helping businesses source produce smarter and reduce costs. It works by allowing farmers to sign up, get verified, access credit for inputs, boost productivity with tools and training, and sell to reliable buyers; businesses use it for traceable produce, AI-powered insights, cost reduction by eliminating middlemen, and seamless transactions.

### How much does UfarmX cost?

UfarmX uses a SaaS subscription model where agribusinesses pay for access to real-time insights, predictive analytics, and traceability tools. It also charges transaction fees on produce sales through its marketplace.

### What countries does UfarmX operate in?

UfarmX operates across Africa.

### How do I sign up for UfarmX?

Farmers and businesses can sign up and get verified directly on the Ufarmx platform.

### Who is UfarmX best suited for?

UfarmX is best suited for smallholder farmers seeking financing, inputs, productivity tools, and market access, and for agribusinesses needing reliable produce sourcing, supply chain visibility, and cost reduction.

### How do I contact UfarmX support?

You can contact UfarmX support by emailing support@ufarmx.com or using the contact form on their Contact Us page on the UfarmX website.

### Does UfarmX have a plan for teams or businesses?

Yes, UfarmX offers SaaS subscriptions and marketplace services designed for agribusinesses.

### Does UfarmX offer an API or integrations?

Yes, UfarmX offers an API designed for banks, cooperatives, and agribusinesses to license their patented, AI-powered credit-scoring technology.
